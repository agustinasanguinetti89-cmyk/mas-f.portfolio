Proprietary methodology

01 · Who I am and how I think

The way I work doesn't come from a tool or an AI experiment: it's the operational expression of a methodology systematically documented in a proprietary Professional Knowledge Base, built as the single source of truth for my professional identity.

"It doesn't aim to tell my story — it documents how I think, how I work, and what evidence supports that way of working."

The base distinguishes three levels of knowledge: facts (verifiable information), patterns (repeatedly observed conclusions), and hypotheses (plausible ideas requiring validation). This principle — never confusing certainty with inference — is what structures the prohibitions and confidence levels of every agent.

  • Identity precedes title
  • Evidence precedes narrative
  • Understanding precedes intervention
  • Each idea exists exactly once

AI is incorporated as an enabler that amplifies analytical and management capacity — not as a change of discipline.

02 · My working method

Work is organized into agents with a clear territory and explicit prohibitions, so no agent encroaches on another's role. It isn't an advanced chatbot: it's a professional work architecture where each agent represents a distinct perspective on the same problem.

Guiding principle: understand before proposing. No agent proposes a solution before understanding the context. Every agent states a confidence level, flags assumptions, and declines to answer when minimum information is missing.

Core rule: every output is tentative until a full diagnosis exists with the actual client or stakeholder.

03 · How it works systemically

The pipeline runs across 6 chained stages, implemented in code (TypeScript + Express), with two trigger modes: full pipeline or concurrent waves.

Stage 0Ingestion & Radiography

Takes in text, audio, PDF or images → produces the 39-cell matrix and the data-quality traffic light.

Wave 1 · ParallelPM + UX + Innovation

Receive the brief → produce the PRD, friction map and innovation diagnosis. In full-pipeline mode, Innovation runs after PM+UX and receives their outputs; in wave mode, it runs in parallel with both.

Wave 2 · ParallelBA + AI Engineer + Data Scientist

Receive Wave 1's output → produce the AS-IS flow, technical architecture and metrics matrix.

Wave 3PMO

Produces the risk matrix, executive summary and EVM governance protocol.

Wave 4The Challenger

Audits every output against Stage 0 → produces a consistency score and verdict.

Wave 5 · ParallelProposal + Public Proposal + Lessons

Close the cycle with the commercial deliverable, the public site and the lessons log.

Standalone mode: any agent can be re-run in isolation, outside the full pipeline, with a specific instruction — useful for point queries or following up on active projects.

05 · The 12 agents in detail

Ingestion & Radiography

Maps the user journey, friction and change resistance before touching the solution.

PMO · International standards

Risk matrix, milestones and EVM governance protocol with weekly SPI/CPI tracking.

// The Challenger

The Challenger

Detects contradictions across the 8 prior agents and issues a consistency score from 0 to 100.

// Proposal

Executive Strategist

Turns the analysis into persuasive communication, backed by the PMO output.

// Public Proposal

Client-Facing Web Version

Generates the interactive public site, free of internal audit language or system references.

Project Closure

Logs which assumption failed and what would change next time. Archived as cross-project history.

06 · Which AI model the system runs on

Product design

The method doesn't depend on any specific AI provider. In the version Nextgenflow delivers to its clients, every agent in the workflow runs on whichever AI model the organization configures, according to its own requirements for quality, cost, privacy and regulatory compliance. Output quality is controlled through each agent's prohibitions, cross-agent auditing and human checkpoints — not through the choice of a specific model.

My current configuration

In my own deployment, all 12 agents run on the Google Gemini family, through Google AI Studio, with a single active model selectable from the interface and an adjustable reasoning level depending on task complexity.

If the selected model is unavailable, my deployment retries automatically with backoff and switches to a model in the same family without interrupting execution. This is a usage configuration, not a product constraint.

07 · Flows by mode of use

// Consulting Mode

Aimed at corporate clients and agencies. Runs the full pipeline with a commercial proposal and its public version for presentation.

// Strategic Application Mode

Uses reverse-engineered job descriptions for AI and digital-transformation leadership applications. No pricing or "first 90 days" framing; authorship is always attributed to me.

Reference runtime in my current configuration: 25–45 seconds through cross-agent audit (no proposal); 60–95 seconds for the full pipeline. The result is a first draft for human review, not a final deliverable.

08 · How context passes between agents

Context isn't copied by hand: each call sends a typed JSON object containing the Stage 0 brief and the relevant prior agent outputs, plus the active model, language and usage-mode parameters.

09 · Input Protocol

Before any data enters the pipeline, it's normalized into a 39-cell matrix organized into 6 blocks: Identity & Sponsorship, Operating Context & Pain, Data Maturity & Security, People & Culture, Objectives & Success Metrics, and Constraints & Critical Assumptions.

Each cell is tagged with confidence: High Medium Low. The overall traffic light blocks progress when the input is ambiguous, contradictory, or pure corporate marketing; in the intermediate zone it requires a leveling questionnaire but allows continuation. A person can manually override the traffic light and unlock progress.

Text, PDF, audio and images are accepted as input. When a search is confidential, the system replaces the client's or intermediary's name with an anonymous descriptor across every public output.

10 · Harness Protocol

Mandatory human checkpoints: brief validation before launching the pipeline, the red-traffic-light gate, and approval of the audit verdict before any proposal is treated as final. No action is ever published or sent to a client automatically.

Resilience (in my current deployment): automatic retries with backoff and JSON repair for truncated responses. If retries are exhausted due to saturation or quota limits, a continuity mode activates, explicitly flagged as such in the output: it generates structured content from the available text instead of stopping the pipeline. In that mode, the cross-audit score does not reflect a real analysis and should be read as provisional.

EVM governance: SPI and CPI are green from 0.95, in alert between 0.85 and 0.94, and in critical deviation below 0.85. The risk score (Low/Medium/High/Critical) automatically escalates one category when data confidence is low.

Cross-agent audit: consistent from 80/100, minor observations between 60 and 79, critical contradictions below 60.

13 · Common pitfalls and how to avoid them

Skipping the Innovation Strategist because "I already know what the client needs" — that's exactly where unidentified ambition gaps appear.

Accepting a verdict without reading the assumptions behind it.

Sharing Innovation's raw internal output with the client unfiltered — it's an internal input; the Public Proposal turns it into appropriate communication.

Starting with a red-light brief without resolving the gaps — the result inherits that fragility at every following stage.

Running Wave 2 before Wave 1 has closed — the three agents would work on a poorly calibrated problem.

Treating a continuity-mode output as a real analysis — defeats the purpose of the resilience protocol.

Jumping straight to Proposal without going through PMO — loses governance and risk backing in front of the client.

Ignoring a critical-contradictions verdict from The Challenger before treating a proposal as final.

14 · Quality checklist before any delivery

Does every brief cell have a source and a classified confidence level?

Is the Stage 0 traffic light green, or yellow with gaps resolved — not red?

Did every agent stay within its territory without invading another's?

Did Innovation receive PM + UX context before issuing its verdict?

Was there a human checkpoint before any irreversible action?

Did The Challenger score consistency at 80 or above, or were observations reviewed?

Does the risk matrix have probability, impact and an owner per item?

Does the Public Proposal omit agent names, models and internal modes?

Were Lessons Learned archived at closure, with an explicit failed assumption?

Was no output marked final if it came from continuity mode?
